# Data attribution

The built-in “AP 备考常用 1000 字” collection is a product-curated study list. It is not an official College Board vocabulary list.

Character frequency ranking is derived from the SUBTLEX-CH-CHR dataset distributed by `subtlex-ch-chr` under the MIT License.

Chinese readings and English dictionary content are derived from CC-CEDICT, distributed by `cedict-json` under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license. CC-CEDICT is available from [MDBG](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict).
